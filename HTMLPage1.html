SCENE 01

Fallen Star

The Quiet Night

The city had gone quiet. Windows glowed softly above empty streets, and the river carried the last reflections of the day. High above the rooftops, one small star shone brighter than the rest.

SCENE 02

The Fall

Something Changed

Without warning, the star slipped from the sky. It crossed the darkness in a trail of gold, falling past the rooftops until its light disappeared somewhere below.

SCENE 03

The Discovery

A Light Between the Stones

At the end of a narrow street, someone noticed a warm glow between the stones. A small star rested there, still shining, as if it had been waiting to be found.

SCENE 04

The Light

The City Wakes

They lifted the star carefully into their hands. Its glow spread across the street, through the windows and over the river, until the sleeping city began to shine again.

Some light falls so the world can shine.
